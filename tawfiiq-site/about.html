Our story

From a single company to a trading group

Tawfiiq was founded in Bosaso in 1992 as one of the first businesses in Somalia organized as a modern company. At the time, most businesses were sole proprietorships or family-owned.

Today Tawfiiq is one of Somalia's main foodstuff distributors and a leading supplier of building materials, electronics, furniture and general merchandise. We are the sole distributor for several producers and a co-investor in Salalah Macaroni Company.
