Our story

A modern Somali company since 1992

Founded in Bosaso in 1992, Tawfiiq was one of the first businesses in Somalia set up as a modern organization — at a time when most companies were sole proprietorships or family-run.

Today we are one of the country's main foodstuff distributors, the sole distributor for several producers, and a co-investor in Salalah Macaroni Company.
